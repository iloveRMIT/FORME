HOW FORME WORKS

Starting with your wardrobe.

What is FORME?

FORME is an occasion-based rental prototype that starts with what you already own, then suggests the missing pieces.

Do I need to rent a full outfit?

No. The idea is to rent only the gap.

How do recommendations work?

They use predefined, rule-based styling logic based on your manually confirmed garment and occasion.

Does FORME actually analyse my photo?

No. Image upload is preview-only in this frontend prototype.

RENTAL

Renting the missing pieces.

How long is a rental?

Prototype prices are for three days.

When will items arrive?

The suggested window is two days before your event through one day after.

Are clothes cleaned?

Every prototype product is marked as professionally cleaned after each rental.

What happens if an item does not fit?

This prototype includes basic size guidance only.

PAYMENT

Prototype booking.

Is the deposit refundable?

The VND 200,000 deposit is labelled refundable subject to return conditions.

Does this prototype process payments?

No. All checkout information and payment fields are simulated.

SUSTAINABILITY

Using what you have.

Why does FORME start with what I already own?

It makes occasional dressing simpler and can prevent a one-event purchase. Style, convenience and appropriateness remain the primary benefits.
