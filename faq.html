HOW FORME WORKS

Occasion first.

What is FORME?

FORME is an occasion-first formalwear rental prototype: a small-scale physical store integrated with a supporting website.

Do I need to add something I own?

No. Adding an owned garment is optional. It can produce complementary recommendations, or you can skip it and request a complete outfit.

How do recommendations work?

They use predefined, rule-based styling logic based on occasion, collection, style direction and any manually confirmed owned garment.

Does FORME analyse uploaded photos?

Not in the current prototype. An uploaded image is a visual reference and garment details are manually confirmed.

RENTAL

At the FORME store.

How long is a rental?

Prototype prices are displayed for three days.

How are items collected?

Customers book a fitting and collect selected rental items at the FORME store.

How are items returned?

Items are returned to the FORME store after the event.

What happens if an item does not fit?

The fitting visit is where customers confirm the selected pieces. This prototype also includes basic size guidance.

THE PROTOTYPE

What is simulated?

Does this prototype process payments?

No. The prototype records a fitting and rental reservation only.

Is delivery available?

Not in the current MVP. Flexible delivery is a possible future development after the store-based model is validated.
