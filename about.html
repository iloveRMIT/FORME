ABOUT FORME

Formalwear should fit the moment, not fill your wardrobe.

FORME makes occasion dressing easier whether you own part of the look or want a complete recommendation.

THE IDEA

A small-scale physical store integrated with the website.

The website helps customers plan, receive recommendations, select rental pieces and book a fitting. The FORME store handles fitting, pickup and return.
