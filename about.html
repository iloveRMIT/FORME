ABOUT FORME

Formalwear should fit the moment, not fill your wardrobe.

FORME was built around a simple observation: many people already own part of the outfit they need. The problem is knowing how to complete it.

THE IDEA

Occasion context, wardrobe awareness, styling support and selective rental work together to make formal dressing feel simpler.
